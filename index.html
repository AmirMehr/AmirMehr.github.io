02 — Approach

One person, the whole lifecycle.

Most of my projects didn't come with a spec. They came with a problem and a deadline. This is the sequence I've run, end to end, more times than I can count.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Sit with the client or stakeholder, ask the uncomfortable questions, and separate what they asked for from what they actually need.

  2. 02

    Design

    Application architecture and database schema — shaped around real workflows, and deliberately built to survive the rules changing later.

  3. 03

    Build

    Frontend, backend and mobile. Full-stack implementation in whatever language or framework the problem actually calls for.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    Payments, maps, Firebase, Telegram bots, mobile SDKs, real-time transport — the messy external edge where most projects live or die.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Shipping to production on shared hosting and self-managed Linux VPS infrastructure: Caddy, SSL/TLS, DNS, Cloudflare CDN, Docker, CI/CD.

  6. 06

    Maintain

    Debugging live systems, evolving the production schema, and adapting to requirements that only became visible after real users touched the thing.
